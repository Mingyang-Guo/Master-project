# Semantic Uncertainty Taxonomy

The Step 3 subtype workflow uses 17 report-level semantic uncertainty subclasses:

1. Modal possibility
2. Graded likelihood
3. Suspicion or concern
4. Suggestive or favoring evidence
5. Compatibility or concordance evidence
6. Diagnostic consideration or candidate inclusion
7. Non-exclusion or cannot-rule-out language
8. Indeterminate or unclear judgement
9. Qualified negative or weak absence
10. Diagnostic alternatives or differential diagnosis
11. Potential association or relatedness
12. Etiology or causal attribution uncertainty
13. Limited evaluation or visibility
14. Artifact or evidence-authenticity uncertainty
15. Further evaluation or follow-up recommendation
16. Clinical context dependence
17. Age-related clinical-significance uncertainty

The taxonomy is applied to cue-sentence units and then mapped back to report level after manual review.
